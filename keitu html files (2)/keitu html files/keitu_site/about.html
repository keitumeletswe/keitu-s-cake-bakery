Our Story

Keitu's Cake and Bakery was founded in 2022 in Kagiso, Gauteng, as a home-based bakery born out of pure passion for baking and creative design. What started as weekend bakes for family and friends quickly grew into a fully fledged small business, fuelled by growing demand from the Kagiso community.

From our very first custom birthday cake to elaborate multi-tier wedding creations, every product that leaves our kitchen is made with love, care, and the finest ingredients. We believe every celebration — no matter how big or small — deserves something beautiful and delicious.

Today, Keitu's Cake and Bakery serves clients across Kagiso, Soweto, and the greater Gauteng area, delivering custom creations that turn special moments into lasting memories.
